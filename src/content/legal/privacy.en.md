## Summary

- **No tracking.** This website does not use analytics, advertising pixels, fingerprinting, or third-party marketing cookies.
- **No database.** The contact form only prepares a message in your browser. Nothing is sent to a Noctem server.
- **You choose.** Optional items are only saved in your browser after you consent, and you can withdraw consent at any time.
- **No data sales.** We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

## 3. Contact form

The contact form works entirely in your browser. When you click “Send through WhatsApp” or “Send through email”, the text is assembled locally and opened in the app you chose, where you can review it and decide whether to send it. The fields are **not** transmitted to or stored on Noctem servers.

From that point on, delivery happens through the service you chose (WhatsApp or your email provider), which has its own privacy policy.

## 4. Data sharing

We only share data when needed for the purposes above:

- **Hosting and CDN provider**, which processes technical access logs as a processor;
- **Proton AG**, our email provider, when you write to us;
- **WhatsApp (Meta Platforms)**, when you choose to talk through that channel;
- **Public authorities**, when required by law or court order.

Links to Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn only take you to those platforms when clicked. No content, script, or pixel from these networks is loaded on this website.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set its own cookies. It uses the browser’s local storage (`localStorage` and `sessionStorage`), which works in a similar way and follows the same transparency and consent rules recommended by the ANPD cookie guidance.

| Name | Type | Category | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `noctem-consent` | localStorage | Necessary | Store your privacy choices and the version of this policy | 12 months. After that, we ask for your choice again |
| `noctem-intro-seen` | sessionStorage | Necessary | Prevent the opening animation from repeating on every page | Until you close the tab |
| `noctem-language-detected` | sessionStorage | Necessary | Prevent repeated redirects based on your browser language | Until you close the tab |
| `noctem-language-choice` | localStorage | Preferences (optional) | Remember the language you chose between visits | Up to 12 months or until you withdraw consent |

**Necessary** items do not depend on consent, because the site cannot work as you requested without them. **Preference** items are only saved if you allow them. When you withdraw consent, they are deleted immediately.

The hosting provider may use strictly necessary security cookies, such as bot protection cookies, which are not used for advertising.

## 6. International transfers

Some providers, such as the email provider (Switzerland), the content delivery network, and WhatsApp, may store or process data outside Brazil. These transfers follow article 33 of the LGPD, based on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards adopted by the providers (ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 19/2024), or when needed to fulfil a request you made in connection with a contract (art. 33, IX).

## 7. Security

We apply technical and administrative measures proportional to the risk (art. 46 of the LGPD), based on OWASP recommendations:

- always-encrypted connections (HTTPS with HSTS);
- a restrictive Content Security Policy (CSP) with no third-party scripts;
- headers against clickjacking, content sniffing, and referrer leakage;
- minimization: we keep no database and no form that stores data on the server.

If a security incident may cause relevant risk or harm, we will notify the ANPD and affected data subjects, as required by article 48 of the LGPD and ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 15/2024.

Found a vulnerability? Write to [hello.noctem@proton.me](mailto:hello.noctem@proton.me) or see our [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) file.

## 8. Your rights

Under article 18 of the LGPD, you may request, at any time and free of charge:

- confirmation that we process your data, and access to it;
- correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
- anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed unlawfully;
- portability of your data to another provider;
- deletion of data processed based on your consent;
- information about the entities we share data with;
- information about the option not to consent and the consequences of refusing;
- withdrawal of consent;
- objection to processing based on other legal grounds when the law is not being followed.

**How to exercise them.** Send your request to [hello.noctem@proton.me](mailto:hello.noctem@proton.me). We may ask for minimal information to confirm your identity and protect your data. We reply immediately in simplified form, or within 15 days with a complete statement, as set out in article 19 of the LGPD.

You may also file a complaint with Brazil’s [National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)](https://www.gov.br/anpd).

## 9. Children and adolescents

This website is intended for businesses and professionals and is not directed at people under 18. We do not knowingly process data from children or adolescents. If this happens, the data will be handled under article 14 of the LGPD or deleted.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website or the law. The date and version below always show the current edition. If a change affects items that depend on consent, we will ask for your choice again.
